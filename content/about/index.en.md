<div style="text-align: justify;">

Researcher with a PhD from the University of Granada, specialized in the variational formulation of stochastic processes. He is the Coordinator of the Quantum Unit at the Technological Center [$\mathtt{FIDESOL}$](https://fidesol.org), professor in the Master’s Degree in Quantum Computing at [$\mathtt{UNIR}$](https://www.unir.net/ingenieria/master-computacion-cuantica/claustro/), and collaborates with the research group on Thermodynamics and Quantum Computation [$\mathtt{FQM-411-TCC}$](https://produccioncientifica.ugr.es/grupos/39756/detalle) at the University of Granada. He advises companies that develop products or services based on the latest fundamental research in quantum computing. He fosters collaboration between academia and industry, standing out at the intersection of computational science and quantum research. His research encompasses machine learning and its secure methodologies, employing both classical and quantum techniques.
